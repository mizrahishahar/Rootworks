# Local

## Description

For local-service businesses, proximity is the opener. A neighbor who noticed them, not a vendor blasting a list: the locality first, then a nearby proof in their own terms, plain and casual, the tone of a referral between locals. Trades smell polish instantly.

## Template

**Touch 1**

> Hey {{first_name}}, saw your {{business}} over in {{city}}, looks like you're doing solid work.
>
> We helped another shop in {{city}} book 15 jobs in their first month. {the offer}.
>
> {the CTA}

**Touch 2**, threaded

> {a second local proof, or a neighborly nudge}
>
> {the offer repeated plainly} {a lighter re-ask}

## Examples

> Hey {{first_name}}, saw your {{business}} in {{city}}. Most owners around here still run on word of mouth.
>
> We helped a similar business book 15 appointments in month one.
>
> Worth a quick chat?
