| [Enrich Phones](rootflows/Enrich-Phones/card.json) | People | POST /webhook/enrich-phones, POST /webhook/waterfall-phones | Finds a phone for every person in a People view (Supersoniq, AI-Ark, LeadMagic, Prospeo) and writes the phone lane; also the contact door the Handlers call for one person. | yes |
| [Sync PlusVibe Leads Data](rootflows/Sync-PlusVibe-Leads-Data/card.json) | People | POST /webhook/launch-sync-pv-leads | Nightly: reads every PlusVibe campaign's leads and writes Campaign Status, Messages Sent, Last Contacted, Bounce Reason and the Campaigns link onto People, so the views know who was contacted. | yes |
| [Verify Catch-alls](rootflows/Verify-Catch-alls/card.json) | People | POST /webhook/verify-catch-alls | Sends the candidates of people on catch-all domains to BounceBan and settles Final Email; fired by Enrich Emails and hourly for every client. | yes |
