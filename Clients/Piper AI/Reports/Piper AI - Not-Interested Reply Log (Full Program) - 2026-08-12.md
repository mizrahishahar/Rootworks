## Signal read

### 1. Nobody is arguing with the offer. Almost nobody is engaging with it.

Sixty of 63 replies are a one-line refusal. **Three** in the whole program gave a reason:

- **Tony Latoria (Director of Preconstruction, Russell)** — already built contract-review prompts and a risk register on enterprise AI, in shared co-work environments. This is the "just use ChatGPT" objection, from the exact buyer, unprompted. It is the single most useful thing any prospect has told us, and the "evolving brain / persistent context" differentiator exists to answer it: their build resets every session, ours does not.
- **Sheila Suppes (BD Manager, Clark)** — "we have an estimating team." The "we already do this" objection; answered by velocity and depth, not replacement.
- **Carla Standfield (Tolunay-Wong)** — the buyer retired.

Across 20,915 sends, three people engaged with the substance. That is not a positioning rejection. It is an attention failure — the message is not being read as being about them.

### 2. A fifth of the people we upset are not general contractors.

**13 of 63 (21%) are outside the ICP entirely:** Sphero (educational robotics toys), LEED Electric (electrical sub), Tropic Mechanical and Hoffman Mechanical (MEP), Baker Group (mechanical), Deem Structural (steel), Columbia River Steel (metal buildings), Toro Engineering and Tolunay-Wong (engineering firms), ABC NorCal (a trade association), T5 Data Centers (a developer), CCI Industrial, Artaic. Two more are Canadian (OCL, Espace) against a US-only ICP.

This is the DiscoLike domain-export contamination showing up on the receiving end. It is not merely wasted volume — every one of these is a sender-reputation cost paid for a lead who could never have bought.

### 3. Seven distinct personalisation failures are visible in the replies themselves.

**Wrong first name (5):** Clune ("my name is NOT Matt so I assume you are not a real person"), Baker Group ("Hey Dick" → David), Pogue ("Hey Penny" → Paul), BHI ("Hey John" → Jake), La Macchia ("Hi Charles" → Charlie), plus Espace's broken casing ("Charles-henri") and ABC NorCal's "Hey Drusilla" → Silla.

**Wrong geography (4):** Diede Construction told it runs K-12 "out of Honolulu... Hawaii" while sitting in Stockton, California. Gardner Builders told it runs healthcare "out of Milwaukee... Wisconsin" while sitting in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Burton Construction told "In Idaho" while sitting in Spokane, Washington. Mason Construction wrote back to say we had aimed Florida copy at a Kentucky company.

**Stale contacts (3):** Roger Hickel retired. Esperanza Shackelford retired. Steven Kessler (Torcon) retired.

The Clune reply is the one to sit with. A wrong merge field did not just lose that lead — it told him the sender was a bot, in writing. Every other lead who spotted the same error and simply deleted the mail is invisible in these numbers.

### 4. The people who are in-persona are saying no too.

Fifteen replies came from precisely the seats we target — Directors of Preconstruction, COOs, Presidents, BD Directors, chief estimators: Latoria (Russell), Ferguson (T5), Shea (OCL), Saunders (Trumbull-Nelson), Clemency (La Macchia), Burnette (Hoar), Brinkman (Sampson), Pfannes (Miller-Davis), Suppes (Clark), Tribble (Deem), Clementi (D&P), Heinen (TerWisscha), Dohn (DOHN), Uffelman (Scott-Long), Allen (CNY).

**This is not a persona problem.** The right people are receiving the mail and declining it. Notably, **Brittian Blaylock, Director of Construction Technology at Edifice** — the exact innovation/tech seat the 08-04 sourcing effort was built to reach — already said no on the old copy, before that build shipped.

### 5. Seven people escalated past "no" to "remove me."

Miller-Davis, A-1, Marker ("unsubscribe"), Sampson ("take me off your list"), Hoffman ("REMOVE ME FROM YOUR LIST"), LEED Electric ("stop"), Mike Smith. Roughly one in nine rejections is an irritation, not a decline. Three of those seven are off-ICP contacts we should never have mailed.

## What this points at

The rejections say the break is **upstream of the message**. Before another angle is written:

1. **Cut the non-GCs at source.** 21% of the people we annoyed could never buy. The DiscoLike CONSTRUCTION category does not separate a general contractor from a millwork shop or a toy company.
2. **Gate on name–email corroboration.** Five confirmed wrong-name sends, one of which cost us the sender's credibility in writing.
3. **Gate on city/state truth.** Four confirmed wrong-geography sends in a play whose entire premise is local coverage.
4. **Answer Latoria's objection in the copy.** It is the only substantive objection we have received twice over, and it is exactly what the positioning was rebuilt to counter — it has never actually been put into an email.
