# Passive

No pitch, no calendar, no urgency. You say you are already working in their space, park an offer they can pull on whenever, and let the conversation go where it goes. The reply is the win, and what happens next is your choice.

## The play

Say you exist, ask for nothing.

- **Disarm first.** "been working with a few {{peer_brands}} in your space lately." You are placing yourself inside their circle, not outside it: no stranger to what they do or who they work with. Personalize by industry, or by location when the offer is local.
- **Kill the urgency out loud.** "no urgency, but if you're ever looking to {dream outcome}". Taking the timeline off the table is what takes the resistance off.
- **Specificity is the whole thing.** Name the actual thing you implemented for an actual peer. "if you're ever looking to sign more clients" is generic enough to die on arrival, and the framework carries nothing else to save it.
- **The CTA is a door, not a calendar.** A case study you could show them, or just a LinkedIn connect for now. Two cheap exits, no booking ask anywhere.
- **Write it like a text.** No greeting, first name and straight in, loose grammar, lowercase. It should read like a colleague, not a sender.
- **A reply is the conversion.** Once they answer you pick the next move: a call, a VSL, a custom lead magnet. Push for a commitment on touch 1 and every one of those is gone.
- **Where it stops.** "your name came up in a conversation with another {{peer_brand}}" pulls harder and is a lie you cannot walk back on the call. Not ours.

## Needs

- Real work in their space: a few named peer brands, or a segment we genuinely serve.
- One case study in or adjacent to their vertical, packaged and sendable.
- One specific implementation, nameable in a single line.
- A LinkedIn profile worth connecting to.
- Base personalization. Vars: `{{peer_brands}}`, `{{case_study}}`.

## Subject lines

There is nothing to open, so keep it flat and internal.

- your space
- no ask, {{first_name}}
- {{industry}} question

## Touches

Two touches.

- **Touch 1** - the presence line and the parked offer. 2 to 4 variants.
- **Touch 2** - the follow-up, the case study offered outright. 2 variants.

## Template

**Touch 1**

> {{first_name}}, been working with a few {{peer_brands}} in your space recently.
>
> No urgency, but if you're ever looking to {the specific outcome}, could always show you what we did for {{case_study}}. Or just shoot a quick connect on LinkedIn for now.

**Touch 2** - the follow-up, body only, threads under Touch 1

> {the case study named with its number, offered outright} {no ask heavier than "want it?"}

## Examples

**Lead-gen (our own offer):**

> {{first_name}}, been running outbound for a few B2B teams in your space recently.
>
> No urgency at all, but if you ever want to add 15 to 20 qualified calls a month without hiring an SDR, could always show you exactly what we built for {{case_study}}. Or just connect on LinkedIn for now.

**Local:**

> {{first_name}}, been working with a few movers around {{city}} lately.
>
> No urgency, but if you're ever looking to get inbound leads quoted the same hour instead of them sitting till the crew's back, could always show you what we set up for {{case_study}}. Or just shoot a connect on LinkedIn for now.
