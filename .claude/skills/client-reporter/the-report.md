# The report

One message, written for the client's channel, sitting next to the numbers message. It does not repeat the numbers table. It quotes a number only when the number is the point of the sentence.

## The shape

| # | Part | Holds | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headline | The week in one sentence, in his terms, with the win inside it | 1 sentence |
| 2 | Done for you | Work finished this week, each as what it did for him | 2 to 4 bullets |
| 3 | Learned and changed | Tried, saw, changed. The steering | 1 to 3 bullets |
| 4 | Next | What happens next week, and anything we need from him | 1 to 3 bullets |

The whole message stays under 150 words. Each bullet is one line, two at the most. If a section has nothing true to say, it is left out that week; it is never padded.

Layout: the headline as plain text on top, then each part under a short bold label, bullets beneath. Labels may change wording week to week ("This week", "What we changed", "Coming up"); the order never does. No emoji in labels, no bold inside sentences, no sign-off paragraph. The last bullet of Next is the ending.

## Each part

**Headline.** The five-second read. It carries the direction of the week and the win. "Second angle is working: 6 positive replies this week against 2 last week, 3 of them already booked." On a weak week it carries the fact and the motion together: "Quiet week on replies, so we moved early: new angle went live Wednesday."

**Done for you.** Finished work only, with a count or a day. Order by what he cares about most: conversations and meetings first, then audience, then copy, then the protective work. The protective work gets one bullet at most.

**Learned and changed.** The heart of the report and the part that shows judgment. Each bullet is one loop: what we tried, what we saw, what we changed. A loop still open says what we are watching and when it will be readable. This is also where a miss lives, stated plainly with its change beside it.

**Next.** Concrete and dated where possible. Anything needed from him goes here as one clear ask with the outcome it unlocks. Whatever is written here is owed an answer in next week's report.

## Week types

| Week | What leads | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ramp-up (nothing sending yet, or just started) | Done for you, then Next | Progress is the product. Break the build into visible steps so each week shows movement. Say when the first results will be readable. |
| Normal | All four parts | The win sits in the headline. |
| Strong | All four parts | Celebrate in the headline, one line, against his own past weeks. Then straight to how we extend it. Say what is being done to repeat it. |
| Miss | Headline with fact and motion, then Learned and changed | The change already made sits beside the miss. Next carries the step after that. If the miss is the second in a row or the why is more than a line, offer a call in Next and note it to the Operator. |

## Worked example, normal week

Second angle is pulling ahead: 6 positive replies this week against 2 last week, and 3 are already on your calendar.

**This week**
- Reached 1,140 new operations leaders at logistics companies between 200 and 2,000 people
- Answered every reply within the hour; 3 booked, 2 asked for details and have them
- Added 800 freshly verified contacts for next week's sends

**What we changed**
- The cost angle got 1 reply from 600 people. The speed angle got 5 from 540. From Wednesday all new sends use speed.
- Directors are replying, VPs are not yet. Next week's list leans toward directors.

**Coming up**
- A third angle built on your new case study goes live Tuesday
- One ask: the two people who wanted pricing are waiting on your side. A reply today keeps them warm.

## Worked example, miss week

Quiet week on replies, so we moved early: a new angle went live Wednesday and the first reads come Monday.

**This week**
- Reached 1,300 finance leaders at software companies; 1 positive reply, no meetings yet
- Rewrote the opening email around the audit deadline your buyers face in Q4
- Kept sending capacity at full strength; every address verified before it went out

**What we changed**
- The savings angle is not landing with this audience: 1 reply from 1,300. We stopped it Wednesday and moved all volume to the deadline angle.
- Too early to read the new one. Monday's report will have its first real numbers.

**Coming up**
- If the deadline angle reads well Monday, we widen it to the 50 to 200 band
- Worth 15 minutes together this week to walk through what we are seeing. I will send two times.
