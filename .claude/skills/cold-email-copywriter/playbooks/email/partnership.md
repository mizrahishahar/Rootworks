# Partnership

## Description

Not a sale, a referral channel. Adjacent, non-competing providers who serve the same ICP from another angle (the CPA to our bookkeeping), principals only. Two angles: adjacent-angle, where the incentive is the reason to talk (two-way referrals or a rev-share); intro-first, where our own client base is the asset and we are handing them an opportunity, so there is nothing to lose by replying. The shared customer has to be real; sent as cover for a reverse pitch it burns the contact. No pitch anywhere. Slow to close, compounding: one partner exposes you to dozens of their clients.

## Template

**Touch 1, adjacent-angle**

> Hey {{first_name}}, we serve the same {{ICP}} you do, just from the {{adjacent_angle}} side.
>
> Curious if there's room for a simple referral partnership, two-way or a rev-share, whichever's cleaner for you.
>
> Worth a quick chat?

**Touch 1, intro-first**

> {{first_name}}, I'm currently working with a ton of {{their_ideal_customer}}.
>
> Thought there could be an opportunity for a referral partnership, happy to set up a few intros?

**Touch 2**, threaded

> {a partner result, or the incentive spelled out}
>
> {the partnership in one line} {a lighter re-ask}
