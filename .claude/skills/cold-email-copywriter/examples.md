Teaches: our own winning sequences, verbatim as deployed, with their numbers and why each one earned its replies.

# Examples

**Everything here is contextualized.** Each sequence was written for one client, one segment, one offer, one moment in the market. Nothing is a template. Read them for inspiration: how a real fact opens, how proof gets bridged to an outcome, how a CTA collects. Never lift a line into a different context. Every sequence here earned real positive replies; the live numbers are in the Hub, always current, never copied here.

## 1. Adelante · UK DTC · Trustpilot Under 4.2 (pitch-led)

The account's best broad-list producer.

**Why it won:** the opener is a number the reader owns ({{trustpilot_rating}}), tied immediately to lost revenue. One argument end to end: rating, lost revenue, agent that answers in seconds, proof (Fold, 100+ inquiries a day), guarantee covering exactly that promise, and a CTA that costs one word because the work is offered already-done, over email. The follow-up adds honest scarcity (only brands in this rating range) and a real deadline (Black Friday) instead of repeating the ask.

### Touch 1

**Variant A**  ·  *Subject:* net revenue at {{company_name}}

> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> I saw {{company_name}} is rated {{trustpilot_rating}} on Trustpilot, which usually means you are losing net revenue to questions nobody answered in time.
>
> We fix that with a proactive AI agent that plugs into your systems and answers every one of them in seconds, so the buyer never leaves to find out.
>
> That is what we built for Fold, a UK brand on 100+ inquiries a day, where every question now gets answered the second it lands.
>
> You will get half of your inquiries solved from day one, or you pay nothing.
>
> I can build you a first version to try, right here over email. Want me to?
>
> P.S. not relevant? just reply "no thanks"

**Variant B**  ·  *Subject:* {{company_name}} online reputation

> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> I saw {{company_name}} is rated {{trustpilot_rating}} on Trustpilot. It became important again, because it is the first source ChatGPT pulls when it recommends a brand like yours.
>
> We handle that for you behind the scenes, with a proactive AI agent that wires into your systems, resolves orders and tickets, and pushes your rating up.
>
> A UK ecom store now resolves 100% of tickets end to end, and their rating climbed from 3.5 to 4.1. So they get seen more when someone asks.
>
> You will get half of your inquiries solved from day one, or you pay nothing.
>
> Mind if I share more info?
>
> P.S. not relevant? just reply "no thanks"

### Touch 2

*Threaded, +3 days · single variant*

> FYI, we only offer this to brands in your Trustpilot range, because that is typically where the most net revenue gets lost.
>
> It is the same reason we guarantee it: half your inquiries resolved from day one, or you do not pay.
>
> We can make it live before Black Friday, when these inquiries are worth the most revenue.
>
> I can build you the initial version now, over email. Want to see it?

> P.S. not relevant? just reply "no thanks"

## 2. Move PLNR · Public 11-50 (pitch-led, founder-as-proof)

The sibling campaign (2026-07-07 Home Movers, Company Inbox) ran the same argument at scale and is the account's volume king.

**Why it won:** the founder IS the proof. Sam is a mover who built the tool scaling his own company, and the delta is felt ("$2M to over $7M, 13 trucks to 51 across 6 cities"). Four variants, four genuinely different angles at the same audience: intro-to-Sam, pain-first, category-claim, operator-specific chaos (dispatch over WhatsApp). Operator language everywhere: trucks, crews, dispatch, double-bookings. Email 3A is the email-tree ask, which keeps earning replies from the right person.

### Touch 1

**Variant A**  ·  *Sam intro*  ·  *Subject:* {{company_name}} x MovePlnr - intro

> Looked up movers in {{city}} and {{company_name}} kept coming up.
>
> Sam owns a moving company too, Cactus Moving. As he added trucks and cities, his tools couldn't keep up, so he built MovePlnr to scale without the chaos.
>
> He took Cactus Moving from $2M to over $7M, 13 trucks to 51 across 6 cities.
>
> He's happy to walk another owner through it. Can I intro you to Sam?

**Variant B**  ·  *Loom*  ·  *Subject:* Leads slipping through?

> At {{company_name}}'s size, I'd bet leads fall between people and crews step on each other some days.
>
> That's revenue leaking out. MovePlnr runs leads, quoting, and dispatch on one board, so you book more and run tighter as you grow.
>
> Cactus Moving grew from $2M to over $7M on it.
>
> Mind if I share a quick video of how it works?

**Variant C**  ·  *More info*  ·  *Subject:* MovePlnr for {{company_name}}?

> Running a growing operation in {{city}}, you've probably hit the point where the tools that worked at 5 trucks don't at 15.
>
> MovePlnr is the one system movers scale on, leads, quoting, dispatch, and payroll in one place, so adding trucks and cities doesn't mean rebuilding everything.
>
> It's what took Cactus Moving from $2M to over $7M across 6 cities.
>
> Mind if I send more info?

**Variant D**  ·  *Loom, varied*  ·  *Subject:* question about {{company_name}}

> Looked up movers around {{city}} and landed on {{company_name}}.
>
> As you run more crews at {{company_name}}, dispatch over WhatsApp turns into double-bookings and no-shows. MovePlnr puts every crew, job, and update on one board, so you scale jobs without scaling the chaos.
>
> Sam ran Cactus Moving to over $7M on it.
>
> Mind if I share the walkthrough he recorded?

## 3. Adelante · Israeli DTC Hebrew · General, No Bot (pitch-led)

The account's best positive rate at meaningful volume.

**Why it won:** it names the reader's exact repeating tickets in their own words (order status, returns, availability), gives one proof with a hard number (Modibodi, ~70% closed with no human), and the CTA is the lightest yes in the stack: reply "yes", get a WhatsApp number, try the agent. The follow-up never repeats the ask; it adds a real seasonal deadline, reframes price against the alternative the reader is actually weighing (hiring a support rep), and closes on a question about THEIR world.

Hebrew ships unspun, wrapped in a `dir='rtl'` container, with {{hebrew_first_name}}, never a transliteration.

### Touch 1

*Subject (Hebrew):* the support of {{company_name}}

> היי {{hebrew_first_name}},
>
> אם רוב הפניות של {{company_name}} חוזרות על עצמן: סטטוס הזמנה, החזרות, זמינות - זה בטח מציף אתכם.
>
> בנינו נציג AI שסוגר את הפניות האלה מקצה לקצה: קורא את הנתונים מה-Shopify שלכם וגם מבצע פעולות אמיתיות כמו בדיקת סטטוס, החזרה והחלפה.
>
> אצל Modibodi הנציג סוגר כ-70% מהפניות בלי נציג אנושי.
>
> רוצים נציג שעובד על האתר שלכם ולפי הנהלים שלכם? אם זה מעניין אותך אפשר לרשום כן ואשלח לך מספר ווטסאפ לנסות אותו.

### Touch 2

*Threaded, +1 day · single variant*

> {{hebrew_first_name}}, בין החגים לנובמבר לא נשאר זמן להטמיע כלום. מי שרוצה נציג מוכן לפיק, צריך להתחיל עכשיו, ותוך שבוע הנציג שלכם באוויר.
>
> במקום לגייס נציג תמיכה ב-8 עד 10 אלף שקל בחודש, זה עולה $800 בחודש, בלי שתתחזקו כלום: אנחנו מפעילים ומשפרים את הנציג בשבילכם כל שבוע.

> מה הפנייה שהכי מציפה אתכם כרגע: סטטוס הזמנה, החזרות או זמינות? בואו נפתור ספציפית את זה קודם.

## 4. Dave.io · B2B Tech US 1-50 · Infra Decision Makers (feedback-led)

v1 underperformed and was retired; v2 below is the live rework. Included as the account's cleanest feedback-led shape and the source of Exhibit A in `writing-rules.md`.

**Why it earns its place:** the founder story is the opener (left monday.com to build), the ask is honest and singular (your take, not a demo), {{authority_line}} is researched personalization that explains WHY this reader's opinion matters, and the risk reversal is a bet with a smile: $200K saved best case, twenty minutes and a $50 gift card worst case. Touch 2 adds honest scarcity (only teams running real infra without a team) and hard proof (8 teams in production, ~45% costs cut).

### Touch 1

**Variant A**  ·  *Subject:* your take {{first_name}}?

> {{first_name}}, my co-founders and I left monday(.)com to build the first ever autonomous cloud infrastructure.
>
> Given the fact that {{authority_line}}, you'd know fast whether this is useful or nonsense.
>
> Would appreciate your honest take.
>
> P.S. Best case I save you $200K a year on an infra role. Worst case I owe you twenty minutes and a $50 Amazon gift card.

**Variant B**  ·  *Subject:* built something for lean eng teams

> {{first_name}}, I'm writing because {{authority_line}}.
>
> We are three founders that left monday(.)com to build the first ever autonomous cloud infrastructure.
>
> I'm looking to get your honest feedback on what we've built. I think it can help you.
>
> Can I share more?
>
> P.S. Best case I save you $200K a year on an infra role. Worst case I owe you twenty minutes and a $50 Amazon gift card.

### Touch 2

*Threaded, +1 day · single variant*

> FYI, I'm only asking teams running real production infra without a real team dedicated to it. That's why you came up.

> I'm pretty sure it'll blow your mind, and might save you $200K a year on an infra role. If not, a $50 Amazon gift card, on me.

> Can I show you what we built?

## 5. LinkedIn · opening a conversation (Dave.io, Question campaigns via Alta)

The Question campaigns are the biggest positive producers on the whole account, any channel.

**Why it wins:** one message, sent only after the connection is accepted. One fact the list already established about this person, one question answerable in one line, first name only, and nothing else. No pitch, no sequence, no link. The reply lands in the inbox and the setter works it live. The full playbook is `playbooks/linkedin/question-kickstarter.md`; these are the four shapes as deployed:

**Who handles it** (the fact is an absence)

> Hey {{first_name}}, noticed you don't have anyone dedicated to infra yet. Who ends up dealing with it when something needs attention, you?

**Open, no assumption** (the fact is public and thin)

> Hey {{first_name}}, saw you're hiring for an infra role. Can I ask how that's going for you?

**Binary, one option loaded** (the fact lands on a person)

> Hey {{first_name}}, saw you've got one person carrying infra right now. How's that going for them, drowning or manageable?

**Confirm, cushioned** (the fact is inferred)

> Hey {{first_name}}, might be off here, but guessing infra lands on whoever's closest when something breaks. Is that about right?

### Spintax

None. LinkedIn messages are one-to-one; variants exist per fact, never per phrasing.
