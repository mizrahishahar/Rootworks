# Signal-led

Something they did is the reason you are reaching out. The signal buys the open, and the whole craft is spending it well: naming it plainly, taking only what it actually licenses, and never showing how you found it.

## The play

Lead with what they did, spend it once.

- **The signal is permission, not a pitch.** It earns the open and nothing more. What you do with that opening is a separate decision, made on how strong the signal is.
- **One line, stated as observation.** The way a person would mention it in passing. The moment it reads as surveillance it costs more than it bought.
- **Two messages, not one paragraph.** The signal lands on its own, then the move. One thought each, sent back to back the way a person actually writes. The second is part of the open, never a follow-up on a timer.
- **Never expose the mechanism.** Say the part that is public to them, never how it reached us. The scrape, the feed, the resolution stays invisible.
- **Spend it once.** Naming the signal a second time turns an observation into a case you are building against them.
- **First name only.** No company token, no role token in the signal line if the signal alone carries it. Merge fields stacked in a short message read as a template no matter how true they are.
- **Question or statement, whichever the signal will bear.** A hard, unambiguous signal carries a statement or an offer. A soft or inferred one carries a question and little else.
- **Aim at the owner of the outcome.** The person attached to the signal is often not the buyer. The recruiter posted the role; a junior triggered the visit.
- **Screen it before you use it.** A stale or wrong signal is worse than none, because the open was the only thing it was worth.

### Reading a signal

Any new signal answers three questions before a line is written. This is the generalized move, and the table below is only it applied.

1. **What it actually tells you.** The literal fact, never the inference. A posted role tells you a seat is open, not that they are in pain.
2. **How much it licenses.** How sharply you may name it, and whether you may go past the question.
3. **What it forbids.** The line that would expose the mechanism, and the false positive that would embarrass us.

| Signal | What it tells you | It licenses | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A role posted** | A seat is open and the budget cleared. The need is public and declared by them. | The most of any signal. Name the role outright and name what is not getting done while it sits open. | Pitching the recruiter. The signal is theirs, the pain is the outcome owner's. |
| **Engagement with a topic** | Interest in a subject. Nothing whatsoever about their own situation. | The topic, framed generically, and a soft question about whether it is live for them. | Naming the creator or the post. Naming who you scraped is creepy and burns the play. |
| **A follow of a page** | Problem-awareness, possibly active evaluation. Public, so safe to say out loud. | Naming the follow, and offering the alternative or the upgrade. | Sending it to anyone inside that company's orbit. |
| **A visit to our site** | Someone at that company looked. Which someone is a guess. | Very little. Split-test transparent against silent; silent usually wins. | Calling out the visit to an unverified person. The false positive is the expensive one here. |

### What the list must guarantee

Copy cannot fix a bad signal, so these are met before a send and they are the list's job, not this file's.

- Decision-maker expansion by company size, with recruiters and HR excluded.
- Current and former employees of a followed company excluded.
- Visitor identity filtered hard to ICP before anyone is contacted.
- Engagers deduped by profile, DNC applied.

## Needs

- A screened signal per lead, fresh and genuine.
- The list guarantees above, met.
- Base personalization: `{{first_name}}`, and nothing else.

## Touches

Two messages, connection-gated, sent back to back.

- **Connection request** - blank.
- **Message 1** - the signal, on its own. 2 variants.
- **Message 2** - the move: a question, an offer, or the gap named. Sent immediately after Message 1, no delay between them. 2 variants.

## Template

**Connection request** - blank

**Message 1** (on accept)

> Hey {{first_name}}, {the signal in one line}.

**Message 2** (immediately after, no delay)

> {the move: a question, an offer, or the gap named}

## Examples

One per signal.

**A role posted**

> Hey {{first_name}}, saw you're hiring for a {{role}}.

> We handle {{function}} in the meantime, so you're not waiting on the hire to get {{outcome}}. Worth a chat?

**Topic engagement**

> Hey {{first_name}}, noticed you've been deep in {{topic}} lately.

> Are you looking at {{outcome}} this quarter, or just following the space?

**A follow**

> Hey {{first_name}}, saw you follow {{page}}.

> Looked at alternatives yet, or still evaluating?

**A site visit, silent variant**

> Hey {{first_name}}, quick one.

> Are you looking into {{problem}} right now, or just early research?
