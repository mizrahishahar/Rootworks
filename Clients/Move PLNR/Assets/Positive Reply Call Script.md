# The Positive-Reply Call, MovePlnr

Word for word. Say it like a person, not a reader.

---

## CADENCE

Call three times back to back on the first attempt, most pick up on the second or third. Nothing, leave a voicemail and text straight after. Then day 2, day 4, day 7, different time of day each time.

**Voicemail and text, same words:** Hey {{first_name}}, {{caller}} from MovePlnr. You and {{sender}} were talking about {{what they answered}}. Wanted to get you a time with Sam. Does {{slot1}} or {{slot2}} work?

Meanwhile {{sender}} keeps it moving on email, fast replies, same thread, same person, twice more that week. Every touch gets logged so nobody doubles up.

---

## OPENING (every call)

**You:** {{first_name}}?

**Them:** Yeah / Speaking / Who's this?

**You:** Hey {{first_name}}, this is {{caller}} with MovePlnr. You answered my colleague {{sender}}'s email about {{what they answered}}, sound familiar?

Now stop and listen. What comes back puts you in one of two lanes.

---

## LANE 1

They sound warm. "Yeah, I meant to get back to her." "Yeah, what's this about?" said friendly. "Sounds good."

**You:** Great, let's get you on with Sam, he's the founder and a mover himself. Does {{slot1}} or {{slot2}} work for you?

**If they ask what happens on the call:**

**You:** He'll walk you through how it'd work at {{company_name}} specifically, the lead side, the quoting, the dispatch. Ask him whatever you want, he runs a moving company himself. {{slot1}} or {{slot2}}?

**When they pick:**

**You:** Perfect, {{chosen_slot}}. I've got {{email}} for you, is that still the best one? ... Great, I'm sending the invite over right now while we're on, you should see it in a second. Sam will meet you on Zoom. Talk soon, {{first_name}}.

**Do not keep selling after they pick a time. Confirm and get off.**

---

## LANE 2

They're positive but not at a meeting. They said yes to the video, or the info, or they're just being polite.

**You:** Yeah, {{sender}} is on it now, she's getting that over to you today. She'll send a couple of times along with it to sit with Sam, our founder, so he can show you MovePlnr itself tied into how you're actually running operations day to day.

**You:** Figured I'd just call and sort it here instead of you going back and forth over email. Does {{slot1}} or {{slot2}} work for you?

---

## THE TWO THINGS YOU'LL HEAR

**"I don't remember that email."**

**You:** No worries. You replied to {{sender}} on {{day}}, she'd written to you about {{one line}}. You came back and said {{their words}}. Ring a bell? ... Either way, quick reason I called...

Then go into Lane 2.

**"We already have a CRM."**

**You:** Yeah, most guys we talk to have something. MovePlnr isn't really a CRM though, it's the operating system for the whole shop.

**You:** What that means for you day to day: every lead that comes in gets quoted same day instead of sitting until someone's back at the office, dispatch runs off one board instead of a group chat, and the payments, the reviews, the follow up all chase themselves. Sam's own company went from 372 moves a month to over a thousand running on it, on the trucks he already had.

**You:** That's what he'll show you. Does {{slot1}} or {{slot2}} work?

---

## RULES

Never send a calendar link. Always two specific times.

Never ask "when are you free."

Never say how long the call is.

Never promise Cactus's numbers as their numbers. Sam's results are his, they're proof it works, not a guarantee.

Send the invite before you hang up.

Every booked call gets a warm up call the day before: "Hey {{first_name}}, {{caller}} from MovePlnr, you're on with Sam tomorrow at {{time}}. Anything you want him to have ready?"
