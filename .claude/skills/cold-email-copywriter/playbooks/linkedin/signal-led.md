# Signal-led

## Description

Something they did is the reason for reaching out. The signal buys the open and nothing more; it is stated once, plainly, as a person would mention it in passing, and never shows how it reached us. Two short messages back to back on accept: the signal alone, then the move. What a signal licenses depends on what it literally says: a posted role is public and declared, so name it and name what is not getting done while it sits open, but write to the outcome owner, not the recruiter; engagement with a topic licenses only the topic and a soft question, never the creator or the post; a follow of a page can be named and answered with the alternative; a visit to our site licenses almost nothing, and the silent version usually wins. A stale or wrong signal is worse than none.

## Template

**Connection request**, blank

**Message 1**, on accept

> Hey {{first_name}}, {the signal in one line}.

**Message 2**, immediately after

> {the move: a question, an offer, or the gap named}

## Examples

**A role posted**

> Hey {{first_name}}, saw you're hiring for a {{role}}.

> We handle {{function}} in the meantime, so you're not waiting on the hire to get {{outcome}}. Worth a chat?

**Topic engagement**

> Hey {{first_name}}, noticed you've been deep in {{topic}} lately.

> Are you looking at {{outcome}} this quarter, or just following the space?

**A follow**

> Hey {{first_name}}, saw you follow {{page}}.

> Looked at alternatives yet, or still evaluating?

**A site visit, silent**

> Hey {{first_name}}, quick one.

> Are you looking into {{problem}} right now, or just early research?
