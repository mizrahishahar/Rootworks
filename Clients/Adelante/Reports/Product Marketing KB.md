# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-07-30*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Adelante CX builds production AI support agents for ecommerce and support teams that need tickets resolved inside real workflows, not generic chat deflection.

**What it does:** Adelante connects to helpdesk, order, shipment, product, policy, and customer systems so AI agents can answer, draft, route, tag, summarize, and take approved actions. The product focuses on controlled workflows with QA, escalation rules, and operational proof.

**Product category:** AI customer support automation, ecommerce support agents, Zendesk AI automation, Shopify support automation.

**Product type:** B2B SaaS / managed AI support service. Adelante builds and operates the agent done-for-you — a product, not an implementation project or consultancy.

**Business model:** Demo-led sales motion. Public CTAs route prospects to book a workflow mapping call.
**Pricing:** Starting at $800/mo, includes 5,000 AI messages, usage-based beyond. No setup fees. No tiers. No free trial. 30-day money-back guarantee. Exception: the Shopify App Store listing offers a $99/500-message self-serve starter tier (pilot ramp) — never reference it outside that listing.
**Launch:** The 10-Day Launch — map/connect (days 1–3), train/test (days 4–7), launch/monitor (days 8–10). A documented product process, never consulting or an "implementation project."
**Compliance:** SOC 2 Type II · GDPR · Shopify App Store · Zendesk Marketplace.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Shopify and DTC ecommerce brands, customer support teams, support operations teams, and teams running Zendesk or adjacent support stacks. Prioritize SMB and mid-market ecommerce brands with painful repetitive workflows.

**Decision-makers:** Heads of CX, support operations leaders, ecommerce operators, founders/COOs at DTC brands, and technical owners responsible for support integrations.

**Primary use case:** Automate high-volume, policy-bound support workflows while keeping high-risk decisions under human review.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Resolve repetitive tickets faster without creating refund, policy, or brand risk.
- Turn messy support threads into clear next steps, summaries, tags, and escalation reasons.
- Prove AI quality by workflow before expanding automation.

**Use cases:**
- WISMO and delivery-status tickets.
- Returns and exchanges.
- Address changes, cancellations, and order edits before fulfillment.
- Damaged, lost, and wrong-item tickets.
- Product recommendation and sizing questions.
- Zendesk routing, trigger cleanup, QA, security, and compliance workflows.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Support teams have repetitive tickets that look simple until they touch fulfillment, refunds, inventory, fraud, warranty, or customer trust.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic AI chatbots answer from help-center content without enough operational context.
- Helpdesk-native AI often summarizes or suggests replies but does not own the end-to-end workflow.
- Manual macros deflect only the simplest tickets and leave agents rebuilding context.
- Broad automation creates risk when refunds, high-value orders, or policy exceptions appear.

**What it costs them:** Slow response times, high support volume, avoidable refunds, reopened tickets, inconsistent policy handling, customer frustration, and lost support-team trust in AI.

**Emotional tension:** Teams want AI leverage but fear embarrassing customer experiences, brand damage, and support agents having to clean up bad automation.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Gorgias AI, Zendesk AI, Intercom AI, Freshdesk AI, HelloRep, Chatbase, and AI support assistants embedded in helpdesk platforms.

**Secondary:** Outsourced support agencies, offshore CX teams, workflow consultants, macros/trigger-based automation, and RPA tools.

**Indirect:** Doing nothing, hiring more agents, building internal scripts, or limiting AI to internal summarization.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Operated, done-for-you model: the customer's team gives direction; Adelante builds, launches, and runs the agent day to day.
- Connected context: order, shipment, product, policy, helpdesk, and customer data.
- Controlled actions: answer, draft, route, tag, summarize, or act only where allowed.
- Human review gates for high-risk, ambiguous, VIP, fraud, compliance, and policy-exception cases.
- QA loop by workflow: accepted, edited, rejected, escalated, reopened, and corrected AI work.

**How we do it differently:** Operated, not installed. Competitors (Gorgias AI, Zendesk AI, Intercom AI) hand teams tools to configure and babysit; Adelante sets up and operates the agent. Closer: "Your team gives direction. Adelante does the operating work."

**Why that's better:** Prospects can start with one painful workflow, measure quality, and expand when the evidence supports it.

**Why customers choose us:** They want automation that closes real tickets while preserving control over policy, customer experience, and margin.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| AI will hallucinate or make policy mistakes | Start with scoped workflows, approved actions, draft-only modes, and escalation rules. Expand only after QA shows reliability. |
| We already have Zendesk/Gorgias/Intercom AI | Helpdesk AI can help, but Adelante focuses on end-to-end workflows connected to order, shipment, product, policy, and operational systems. |
| Our tickets are too nuanced | The workflow map separates repeatable decisions from judgment calls so AI handles what is safe and routes what is not. |
| We do not have clean data | Start with a narrow workflow where the required systems and fields can be connected and audited. |

**Anti-persona:** Teams looking for a generic chatbot, low-quality content deflection, or broad automation without operational ownership.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Repetitive tickets, support backlog, AI tools that only suggest replies, messy Zendesk automation, inconsistent refund or return handling.

**Pull:** Faster resolution, workflow control, connected context, support-agent leverage, and measurable QA.

**Habit:** Manual macros, hiring more agents, helpdesk-native AI defaults, and reluctance to touch old trigger stacks.

**Anxiety:** Brand risk, hallucinations, data access, refund mistakes, agent adoption, and proving quality to leadership.
