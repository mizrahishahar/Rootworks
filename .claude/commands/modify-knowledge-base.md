# modify-knowledge-base

I'm dropping something in: a file, a text, a call takeaway, a ruling, whatever.

$ARGUMENTS

Work out what it is and what it changes, tell me your read in one line, and put it where it lives: the client's KB rows, their overrides, a system's `tools/` file, or a skill or command. Update in place, never duplicate; when a new ruling contradicts an old line, the new one wins. If a number clashes with something marked Verified, ask me which is true.

Close by showing me what changed, where.
