# Partnership

Not a sale, a referral channel. Reach adjacent, non-competing providers who already serve your ICP from another angle: a CPA to your bookkeeping, a designer to your dev. The ask is a relationship, and it compounds.

## The play

Peer to peer, never a pitch.

Open on the shared market from a different angle ("we serve the same {{ICP}} from a different angle"), as equals trading, not selling. The mutual fit in one line and the incentive, with rev-share lowering the friction to say yes. Keep it a quick chat, no deck. Runs evergreen: nurture the partners every 30 days and the deal flow compounds.

Two angles on the same play, and the difference is where the leverage sits.

- **Adjacent-angle.** You both serve the same ICP from different sides, and the incentive is the reason to talk: two-way referrals, or a rev-share.
- **Intro-first.** Our own client base is the asset. "I'm working with a ton of {{their_ideal_customer}}, thought there could be an opportunity for a referral partnership, happy to set up a few intros." This flips the power dynamic. You are not asking for their time or their money, you are handing them an opportunity, so there is nothing to lose by replying.

Both hold to the same guards.

- **The shared customer type has to be real.** Intro-first only runs where we genuinely serve their ideal customer. Sent as cover for a reverse pitch, it lands badly and burns the contact.
- **Principals only.** A referral relationship is agreed by an owner, never by a rep.
- **No pitch anywhere in it.** Short and plain, the way a colleague texts. Never "we do X, Y and Z".
- **Price the tradeoff.** Reply intent runs low if what you need is to close this month. Partners are seeds: one call can expose you to 20, 30, 50 of their clients, which is the whole point and also why it is the wrong play under a fast-close deadline.
- **Where the reply goes.** Two options once they answer, a real partnership or closing them as a client yourself. Lean authentic; the channel is worth more than the one deal.

## Needs

- Adjacent-angle: a list of adjacent, non-competing providers serving the same ICP, principals only.
- Intro-first: a client base that genuinely matches their ideal customer, and intros we can actually make.
- A decided incentive: two-way referrals, or a rev-share.
- Vars: `{{adjacent_angle}}`, `{{their_ideal_customer}}`.

## Subject lines

Peer-toned, partnership-framed.

- referral partnership, {{first_name}}?
- same {{ICP}}, different angle
- few intros for you

## Touches

Two touches.

- **Touch 1** - the peer partnership ask. 2 variants.
- **Touch 2** - the follow-up, a partner result or the incentive again. 2 variants.

## Template

**Touch 1, adjacent-angle**

> Hey {{first_name}}, we serve the same {{ICP}} you do, just from the {{adjacent_angle}} side.
>
> Curious if there's room for a simple referral partnership, two-way or a rev-share, whichever's cleaner for you.
>
> Worth a quick chat?

**Touch 1, intro-first**

> {{first_name}}, I'm currently working with a ton of {{their_ideal_customer}}.
>
> Thought there could be an opportunity for a referral partnership, happy to set up a few intros?

**Touch 2** - the follow-up, body only, threads under Touch 1

> {a partner result, or the incentive again} {a lighter re-ask}
