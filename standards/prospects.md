# Prospects

A prospect is one company, at one client, that answered us or booked. The goal is a meeting held on the client's calendar.

## Statuses

- **Engaged:** LinkedIn only. A person answered without a yes. We answer back; it is a conversation, not a lead
- **Positive Reply:** he said yes to something. In play toward a call
- **Holding:** he named a later time. Out of play until Next Touch Date
- **Scheduled Call:** a meeting is on the client's calendar
- **No Show:** the slot passed and no meeting was held
- **Call Completed:** held. The client's from here
- **Disqualified:** not a fit
- **Lost:** the conversation ran out

A no on email is a DNC and makes no prospect. A no on LinkedIn is Engaged: we get back to him, and we may be the ones who close it.

## Who moves what

- **the handler:** Engaged, Positive Reply or Disqualified at the reply; Scheduled Call at the booking
- **the manager:** Lost, No Show, Call Completed from a recording, Holding back to Positive Reply, a Lost who writes again back in play
- **a person:** Engaged to Positive Reply, Holding with its date, every other move
- a comment on the row overrides the clock

## The clock

Business days, his timezone.

- **He writes:** a reply is due the same business day, to everyone who answered. To a Positive Reply it carries two real times and the scheduling link, both, always
- **The chase,** Positive Reply only, from our last unanswered message: day 1 lost-times, day 2 value, day 4 lost-times and the phone nudge to the client's BDR, day 10 Lost
- **The two moves:** lost-times is the times offered are gone, two new ones and the link. Value is one true thing about his business and why the call pays. They alternate
- **Engaged:** never chased. Day 10 with nothing from him: Lost
- **Holding:** on Next Touch Date he is Positive Reply and a value move is due
- **Booked:** the day before, one note: the link and what the call covers
- **No show:** no meeting held two hours past the slot. The note goes the same day, and the chase runs from it

## Who writes

- the manager decides the move and the day; the agent writes the words; a person approves
- the lost-times move, the day-before note and the no-show note go without approval only for a client the Operator switched on
- a time in a message is a real open slot from the client's scheduler

## The numbers

Per client, trailing 30 days: positive to booked, booked to held. Engaged never counts as a positive.
