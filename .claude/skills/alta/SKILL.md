# Alta

AI-SDR sequencer and inbox, LinkedIn-first with intent email on the side. **For us it is a sequencer and an inbox, never a sourcing tool.** Leads arrive from n8n, Alta enriches and sequences them, and every reply comes back to be worked by hand.

## The laws here

- **Draft, show, wait.** Every message shown and approved before it sends. No exceptions.
- **Pause a campaign before working its threads.** Sequences fire on top of manual replies; a prospect who just met a human must not meet a bot an hour later.
- **Never trust a person record without the thread text.** Names, companies, and employers come back stale; nothing from a record is merged into a message unverified.
- **One retry on a transient error, then stop and ask.** Never a loop.
