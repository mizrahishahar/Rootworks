# followup

Catch up with every prospect, across all our clients, who engaged in the last seven days.

Load `inbox-manager` first; it owns every move here. Pull the prospects from the Hub (engaged or touched within the last seven days, all clients), and work them one by one, oldest silence first: present the lead card (who, which client, where the conversation stands, the thread), then draft the appropriate next move for that conversation, a follow-up per the chase, a reply if the ball is ours, or a "leave it, not due yet" with the reason. One lead at a time, draft shown and approved before anything is sent, then the next.
