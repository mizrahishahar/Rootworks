# Case-study-led

## Description

A peer's result, and nothing else. The offer is never stated; the proof replaces the pitch and the reader draws the conclusion. Match beats magnitude: a modest number from a real peer outperforms a big number from a stranger. Numbers plain, as a delta, no adjectives. One line points at the lever without handing it over. The ask is the artifact (the breakdown, an intro to the owner), never a call; a named owner offering an intro is the strongest version. The follow-up is the next number from the same case. No greeting where there is no name: these land in role inboxes.

## Template

**Touch 1**

> {the peer and the result, in plain numbers}
>
> {one line on what actually changed}
>
> {the artifact offered: the breakdown, or an intro to the owner}

**Touch 2**, threaded

> {the next number from the same case} {the artifact offered again, lighter}

## Examples

**No name, the breakdown**

> Cactus Moving grew from $2M to $7M without adding trucks first.
>
> Most of it came from fixing how the office handles leads, not the moves themselves.
>
> The owner wrote down what he changed. Want the breakdown?

**Named owner, the intro**

> Sam grew Cactus Moving from $2M to $7M on the same trucks, then turned what he changed into a system other movers now run and grow on.
>
> He's offered to walk other owners through it. Want an intro?

**Touch 2**

> Turns out the same fix took Cactus from 372 moves a month to over 1,000.
>
> Happy to send over how they did it, if useful.
