# Asset-led

## Description

The email sells one thing: permission to send something. One asset, built once, sent to everyone; it carries the offer, the proof, the mechanism and the way to book, which is exactly why the email gets to be one line. Never the link in the first email: the yes is the small commitment that gets the asset watched. No calendar link. Written like a text. The mechanism it explains has to be worth hearing about, or the yes is earned and the asset never opened. Most replies of any play, least intent per reply: a volume instrument.

## Template

**Touch 1**

> {{first_name}}, {what the asset is} covering {the mechanism, or the credibility}.
>
> {the ask to send it}

**On reply**

> {the link}
>
> {one line: everything is in there}

**Touch 2**, threaded

> {one lever: the outcome named plainly, the asset made smaller, or what makes them eligible}
>
> {the same ask, softer}

## Examples

**Credibility-led**

> {{first_name}}, I put together a video covering the growth framework I built to take {{peer}} from $5m to $15m in three years.
>
> Mind if I send it to you?

**Mechanism-led**

> {{first_name}}, recorded a two-minute video explaining the system we built to find and book your buyers while they are complaining about competitors publicly.
>
> Do you mind if I send it?

**Touch 2, the outcome named**

> {{first_name}}, worth saying plainly: it's how we {the outcome}, {at no cost until it works}.
>
> Still happy to send it over.
