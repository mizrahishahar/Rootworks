# Offer-led

## Description

The offer is the email. Not the main service, which is too much to ask a stranger for, but a free first slice of the real work, spelled out so concretely that the only reaction is why not. A deliverable they receive, never a diagnosis of their business. Name the four things we would actually do; visible effort on our side reads as effort removed from theirs. Risk goes casually ("free until it works", "no hard feelings if not a fit"), the criteria for who gets it are said out loud, and the ask is for interest, never for time.

## Template

**Touch 1**

> {{first_name}},
>
> {optional: one line naming the shift, or the pain they are living}
>
> If we could {the free deliverable, spelled out} {with only X of their time, or before any commitment}, {the ask for interest}

**Touch 2**, threaded

> {one lever: the offer standing alone, or what makes them eligible}
>
> {the same ask, softer}

## Examples

**Free creatives**

> {{first_name}},
>
> Most brands are still bleeding budget on creative built for the old system.
>
> If we could ideate, design, script and edit five ad creatives for you completely free, with 10 to 15 minutes of your time, would that be worth sharing more?

**A list of their buyers**

> {{first_name}},
>
> If we could put together a list of the {{ICP}} in your market who are actively looking for {the thing they sell}, and hand it over whether or not we work together, would that be worth sharing more?

**Touch 2, the criteria**

> Worth saying: we only do the free build for brands already spending, since that is where it actually proves anything. That is why you came up. Still happy to run it if useful.
