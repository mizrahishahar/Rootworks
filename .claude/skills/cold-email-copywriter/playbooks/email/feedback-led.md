# Feedback-led

## Description

Not a pitch. You tell a respected operator what you built and ask for their blunt take. Nothing to refuse, so the guard never goes up. It needs no proof because the build is the proof, and it only runs when the build is genuinely not a commodity; on a commodity offer it reads as a trick. Two lines: the build, then the ask. Optionally a credential of ours in front, and the personalization is why this person's read carries weight (their position, what they run), never their posts. Zero commitment anywhere. Reply intent runs lower than a direct offer; the conversation is steerable once open.

## Template

**Touch 1**

> {{first_name}}, {optional: who we are, the credential that earned the right to build it} we built {the thing, stated as a build}.
>
> {why their read carries weight}
>
> {the ask for their take}

**Touch 2**, threaded

> {a concrete detail: what it does, who already runs it, or what makes them one of the few being asked}
>
> {the same ask, softer, or an offer to just send it}

## Examples

**Our own offer**

> {{first_name}}, we built a system that books qualified sales calls for B2B teams and only charges per call that actually shows up.
>
> You've been selling into this market longer than most, so your read would mean something here.
>
> Would appreciate your blunt take if you're open to it.

**With the credential, and the bet in the P.S.**

> {{first_name}}, my co-founders and I left monday(.)com to build the first ever autonomous cloud infrastructure.
>
> Given the fact that {{authority_line}}, you'd know fast whether this is useful or nonsense.
>
> Would appreciate your honest take.
>
> P.S. Best case I save you $200K a year on an infra role. Worst case I owe you twenty minutes and a $50 Amazon gift card.

**Touch 2**

> FYI, I'm only asking teams running real production infra without a real team dedicated to it. That's why you came up.
>
> 8 teams are already running it in production. We cut their cloud costs around 45%.
>
> Can I show you what we built?
