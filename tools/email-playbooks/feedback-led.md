# Feedback-led

Not a pitch at all. You tell a respected operator what you built and ask for their blunt take. There is no commitment to refuse, so the guard never goes up. It only runs when the thing you built is genuinely not a commodity.

## The play

Ask for the take, not the call.

- **Two lines is the whole email.** Line one is the offer stated as a build: "I just built {a tool / a system} that helps {{ICP}} {dream outcome}". Line two is the ask: their blunt take, if they are open to it. Nothing else goes in.
- **The build has to be real and uncommon.** A unique mechanism, a piece of software, a system new to their market. On a commodity offer this reads as a trick and dies.
- **Play to their position, not their posts.** The personalization slot is the authority line: why this person's read carries weight, specific to what they actually run or the space they operate in. Never "saw your post", never "noticed you're hiring".
- **Zero commitment anywhere.** No calendar, no terms, no guarantee. It reads like a prototype being shown around, because that is what it is.
- **Peer to peer.** Short, plain, the way you would text a colleague you respect.
- **Price the tradeoff.** Reply intent runs lower than a direct offer, because not everyone who answers is a buyer. Earning the first reply is the hard part, and the conversation is steerable once it is open.
- **Where the reply goes.** One of two ways. They take the call, and the 30 minutes turns into discovery and then the pitch. Or they ask you to send it, and you answer with a lead magnet or a Loom and work back toward the call. The call is always the end goal.

## Needs

- Something genuinely built and uncommon: a tool, a system, a mechanism the market has not seen.
- One dream outcome it produces, named in the prospect's language.
- A true reason this person's read carries weight: their position, their space, what they run.
- An operator who can turn a feedback call into a discovery call.
- Base personalization: `{{first_name}}`. Var: `{{authority_line}}`.

## Subject lines

Small and personal, carrying no pitch at all.

- your take {{first_name}}?
- built something for {{industry}}
- worth 2 min of your time?

## Touches

Two touches.

- **Touch 1** - the build and the feedback ask. 2 variants.
- **Touch 2** - the follow-up, one more concrete detail, the ask lighter. 2 variants.

## Template

**Touch 1**

> {{first_name}}, I just built {a tool / a system} that helps {{ICP}} {dream outcome}.
>
> {the authority line: given what they run, or their position in the space}
>
> Would appreciate your blunt take if you're open to it.

**Touch 2** - the follow-up, body only, threads under Touch 1

> {one concrete detail about what it does, or who it is already working for} {a lighter re-ask, or an offer to just send it over}

## Examples

**Lead-gen (our own offer):**

> {{first_name}}, I just built a system that books qualified sales calls for B2B teams and only charges per call that actually shows up.
>
> You've been selling into this market longer than most, so your read would mean something here.
>
> Would appreciate your blunt take if you're open to it.

**B2B SaaS (MovePlnr):**

> {{first_name}}, I just built a system that quotes an inbound moving lead the same hour it lands, instead of it sitting until the crew is back.
>
> You run one of the bigger fleets in {{city}}, so you'd know fast whether this is useful or nonsense.
>
> Open to giving me your honest take?
